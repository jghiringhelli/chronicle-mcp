## When this license is required

The Software is published under the [PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0](LICENSE).
That license is **free** for individuals, research, and small businesses (organizations
with **fewer than 100** people **and less than $1,000,000 USD/yr** in revenue).

An organization that **exceeds either threshold** needs this Commercial License to use
the Software for its business. This agreement supplements the public license and lifts
the small-business limitation for the Licensee, in exchange for the fees below.
